CBD shop Vienna

Your CBD shop in Vienna-Josefstadt

Cannaplace 1080 is your specialist CBD store at Josefstädter Straße 56 in Vienna’s 8th district. We keep a carefully chosen range: CBD oils with 5 to 20% CBD, aromatic CBD flowers, nourishing CBD cosmetics and practical accessories such as grinders and storage jars.

For us, quality means transparency. Every product comes with a certificate of analysis from an independent lab that documents the cannabinoid profile and THC content and rules out pesticides and heavy metals. You’ll find the values of the current batches on our lab reports page.

In store we take time for your questions — honest, easy to understand and without health claims. CBD oils, cosmetics and accessories can also be ordered online; we deliver discreetly anywhere in Austria. CBD flowers, however, are only available in our store: since 2025, hemp flowers fall under Austria’s tobacco monopoly and may not be shipped. We explain the details in Is CBD legal in Austria?

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Cannaplace CBD shop in Vienna?

Our store is at Josefstädter Straße 56 in Vienna’s 8th district (Josefstadt). By public transport, take tram line 2 or the U6 (Josefstädter Straße station).

When is the CBD shop open?

Monday to Friday from 10:30 to 19:00 and Saturday from 11:00 to 17:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Is CBD legal in Austria?

Yes. CBD products with a THC content of no more than 0.3% are legal in Austria. CBD oils are sold as aroma oils, and since 2025 CBD flowers fall under the rules of the tobacco monopoly.

Can I order CBD online?

We ship CBD oils, cosmetics and accessories anywhere in Austria. CBD flowers are sold in store only, because shipping them is not permitted by law.

Does CBD get you high?

No. CBD is not intoxicating. The high comes from THC — and our products contain less than 0.3% THC.
