What to look for when buying CBD oil

  • Certificate of analysis: it should come from an independent lab and match the batch number on the bottle. Our guide to reading a certificate of analysis explains how.
  • THC content: Austria’s legal limit is 0.3%.
  • Amount in mg: reputable sellers state the CBD amount per bottle as well as the percentage.
  • Carrier oil: hemp seed oil is a proven carrier oil for CBD extracts.
  • Trustworthy advice: beware of health claims — they are not permitted for CBD products.

Why CBD oils are sold as aroma oils in Austria

In the EU, CBD extracts count as novel foods and have not yet been authorised as food or food supplements. That’s why CBD oils in Austria are sold as aroma oils and labelled “not intended for consumption”.

Frequently asked questions

What does 10% CBD mean?

10% means a 10 ml bottle contains roughly 1,000 mg CBD. The exact values for the current batch are in the lab report.

Why does the label say “not intended for consumption”?

CBD extracts are not yet authorised as food in the EU (novel food). In Austria, CBD oils are therefore sold as aroma oils and labelled accordingly.

How should I store CBD oil?

Cool, dark and tightly closed, so aroma and ingredients last longer. The best-before date is printed on the bottle.
