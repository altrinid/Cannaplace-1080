Buying CBD flowers in Vienna: in store only

Following a ruling by Austria’s Supreme Administrative Court in January 2025, smokable hemp flowers are treated as monopoly goods. They may be sold in tobacconists (Trafiken) and — with a transitional licence until the end of 2028 — in specialist hemp stores, but they may not be shipped. That’s why our CBD flowers are only available in our Josefstadt store. Read more in Is CBD legal in Austria?

How to recognise high-quality CBD flowers

  • Lab report: a current certificate of analysis shows the batch’s CBD and THC content and rules out pesticides and heavy metals.
  • Appearance: well-dried flowers are compact, neatly trimmed and show clearly visible resin glands (trichomes).
  • Aroma: a clear, strain-typical aroma indicates careful drying and storage.
  • Storage: flowers keep their aroma longer in airtight, light-proof jars — such as our UV storage jar.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order CBD flowers online?

No. Hemp flowers fall under Austria’s tobacco monopoly and mail order is not permitted. Our CBD flowers are available exclusively in our store at Josefstädter Straße 56.

Are CBD flowers legal in Austria?

Yes, provided the THC content is no more than 0.3%. Since 2025, sales follow the rules of the tobacco monopoly: flowers may be sold in tobacconists and, until the end of 2028, in licensed specialist hemp stores.

How much THC do your CBD flowers contain?

All strains are below the legal limit of 0.3% THC. The exact value for the current batch is in the lab report on each product page.

How old do I have to be to buy CBD flowers?

Like tobacco products, we only sell hemp flowers to persons aged 18 and over. Please bring photo ID.
