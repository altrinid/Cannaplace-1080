Finding the right grinder

  • 2-part or 4-part? A 2-part grinder is compact and quick to clean. 4-part models add a sieve and a compartment that collects fine pollen.
  • Wood or metal? Wooden grinders feel warm in the hand, metal grinders are especially sturdy and easy to clean.
  • Diameter: 50 mm fits in any pocket; from 60 mm you get more room and a better grip.

Storing herbs properly

Light, air and heat make aromas fade. Airtight jars in a cool, dark place are ideal. Violet glass, as used in our UV storage jar, also filters out most light.

How to clean your grinder

  1. Take the grinder apart completely and remove residue with a soft brush.
  2. Soak metal parts in warm water with a little washing-up liquid or in isopropyl alcohol.
  3. Clean wooden parts dry or with a slightly damp cloth only — never soak them.
  4. Let all parts dry completely before reassembling the grinder.

Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between 2-part and 4-part grinders?

A 2-part grinder consists of a lid and a grinding chamber. 4-part grinders add a collection chamber with a sieve and a pollen catcher.

How often should I clean my grinder?

It depends on how often you use it. At the latest when the thread becomes hard to turn, it’s time for a clean.
