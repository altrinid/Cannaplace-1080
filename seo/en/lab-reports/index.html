What we have tested

  • Cannabinoid profile: CBD, CBG and other cannabinoids with exact content.
  • THC: every batch is below the legal limit of 0.3%.
  • Contaminants: pesticides, heavy metals and residual solvents.

How to read the values

Percentages refer to the weight or volume of the product. “Not detected” means a substance is below the lab’s detection limit. Our guide How to read a certificate of analysis explains everything step by step.

We’re happy to show you the full PDF certificate in store or send it on request — just give us a call.

Frequently asked questions

How often are your products tested?

Every batch is analysed by an independent lab. We update the values on this page with every new batch.

Why does the CBD value differ slightly from the label?

Hemp is a natural product and measuring methods have tolerances. Small deviations of a few tenths of a percent are normal.
